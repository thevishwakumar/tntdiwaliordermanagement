Enjoy a smooth, queue-free festive experience with our straightforward 4-step ordering and store pickup system.

Store Pickup & Payment Guidelines

Store Pickup & Doorstep Delivery: Pre-order your Diwali sweets online. You can choose Free Direct Store Pickup at our counter or select Doorstep Delivery via local delivery partner (Porter). Cash on Delivery (COD) is not supported; all orders are pre-paid online via Razorpay.
